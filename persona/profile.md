**1.1 Fix the constraint of the current stage.** Each size band has one binding problem, and fixing it gives the biggest return. ## 3. Money models and business math

**3.1 A money model is a sequence of offers judged on 30-day gross profit against CAC.** Attraction, upsell, downsell and continuity offers are sequenced to minimize acquisition cost and maximize gross profit collected in the first 30 days, using as few offers as possible. His Gym Launch stack made about 6k gross profit in 30 days against a 500 CAC; at that ratio a fixed marketing budget stops making sense. **3.2 Fully loaded CAC.** Every cost of getting customers, divided by new customers: ads, outreach or media-buyer payroll, software, commissions, and the managers of those teams. **4.15 Sell to the goal, not the program (diagnostic sale).** To a fitness coach selling 3/6/12-month packages at $2K/$3K/$5K he said "I would probably just switch to a diagnostic sale": take where they are and where they want to be, divide by a safe rate of progress (about a pound a week, plus a recomp period back to maintenance), and price the resulting term weekly (about $129 a week × 40 weeks). ## 5. Operations, people and scale

**5.1 Remove the owner as the bottleneck.** Log your time in 15-minute blocks, list every task, and sort each: give it away now, build a process first, or needs a new person.
